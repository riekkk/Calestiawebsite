Japan Visa Requirements

Standard checklist for a Japan visa application. Additional documents may be requested depending on your situation.

1

Philippine Passport

2

Visa Application Form (we will provide the form)

3

Photo (passport size, no bangs, visible ear, no earrings, not AI/edited)

4

Birth Certificate (issued within 1 year; not needed if you have a previous Japan visa)

5

Marriage Certificate (issued within 1 year; not needed if you have a previous Japan visa)

6

Itinerary in Japan (we will send the format)

7

Applicant's Personal Bank Certificate (Bank Statement if a Bank Certificate can't be provided, together with an explanation letter as to why)

8

Income Tax Return (provide an explanation letter if you can't provide one). For employed applicants: Certificate of Employment and ITR

9

For business owners: DTI, BIR, Mayor's Permit, ITR

10

For student applicants: photocopy of the student ID and Certificate of Enrollment
